📐 Design rationale

  • Variant A · Round FAB · Apple iOS Camera button + Snapchat ghost-button DNA · single round shape, no copy on the button itself · pure visual focal point · the label sits BELOW so the button is the icon. 96×96 with a pulsing ring = unmissable. Gradient mint→teal→cyan = energetic but still on-brand. Bounce + slight rotate on tap = playful. Best for: visceral first-impression · "what does this DO?"
  • Variant B · Big Playful Pill · Apple Music "Listen Now" card + IG Stories highlight · two-line copy + emoji left + arrow right · gentle tilt animation on idle (eye-catcher without being annoying) · cream/mint gradient soft and friendly. The 56px white emoji circle bobs subtly. Best for: literal clarity · the action AND the promise are stated
