The corrections from v2 → v3

Steve Jobs: "Start with the customer experience and work backwards to the technology"
🪞

Mental-model match

v2 borrowed from Bloomberg/Apple Music — list-based, time-anchored. Wrong vertical. v3 borrows from Booking.com/Airbnb — photo-led, container-anchored. Right vertical.

📸

Photo as primary signal

For trust purchases, photo carries the cleanliness/ambience verdict. Tiny 1:1 thumbnails (current) or 46×46 row anchors (v2 Apple Music) under-deliver. v3 D goes 16:10, ~180px tall · enough to read the space.

🕐

Time = filter, not headline

"ว่างวันนี้ ▾" sits at top as a filter control. Customer picks the window once, shops that match remain. No slot dumping. No "100 slots available" noise. Same as Booking.com setting check-in/out.

🚮

Hide failure entirely

Shops with no slots in selected window don't appear. No "(12 ร้านที่เต็มแล้ว) ›" expander. No grey-out. No count. The customer's feed contains only actionable shops. Per Art's lock.

⏱

Reveal slots, don't commit

Card shows next 3 slots inline ("19:05 · 19:35 · 20:05 คืนนี้") · enough to verify availability matches intent · but tap goes to shop detail, where commitment happens with full context (photos · services · officers · all slots).

🎯

Variant theme as a dot, not a chip

Shop's variant theme (โปร่ง · หรูหรา · etc.) shows as a single 7px colored dot next to the name. Subtle differentiator. Customer learns to recognize "the orange dot is หรูหรา" over time. Like Twitter's blue check or Spotify's playlist colors.
