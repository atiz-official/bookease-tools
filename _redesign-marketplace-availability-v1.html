The 7 deltas · what changed and why

Each change is information-density-positive · removes a tap or removes a guess
01

🟢 Availability pill on photo

Top-left, blurred-white background, time visible. Customer reads "ว่าง 19:05" before consciously deciding to look.

Before: No signal · After: Decision-ready glance
02

📏 Distance pill on photo

Bottom-right, dark glass. "0.8 km" answers "ใกล้ฉันไหม?" without opening Maps or filtering.

Before: Hidden in shop detail · After: On card
03

💰 Starting price visible

"฿300+" in tabular nums. Filters budget customers in/out instantly. No surprise at checkout.

Before: Discoverable only after 3 taps · After: Card
04

⚡ 3 quick-pick slot chips

Tappable. First chip = primary (black). 1 tap → straight to /payment with slot prefilled. Service detail page becomes optional.

Before: 3 taps to reach slot picker · After: 1 tap to commit
05

📊 Sort by availability

🟢 shops bubble to top automatically. ⚫ full shops sort to bottom, collapsed under "(12 ร้าน) ›" expander.

Before: Alphabetical/random · After: Actionable-first
06

🎯 "ว่างตอนนี้" default filter

Pre-selected green pill at top. The default view answers the most common intent ("where can I go now") without filtering.

Before: "ทั้งหมด" default (noise) · After: "ว่างตอนนี้" default (signal)
07

🚮 No more dead-end "เต็มแล้ว" flash

Loading skeleton on the chip itself, not a full-page banner. If full → ⚫ pill on card + collapse, never a wasted click.

Before: 800ms flash of "full" · After: 0ms · never seen

What would Elon do?

First principles applied to the marketplace card
🚮

"The best part is no part"

The shop-detail page is no longer required for booking. It still exists for browsing photos/reviews, but it's not in the critical path. Funnel deletes 1 entire screen.

⚡

"The best process is no process"

The 5-step booking process (home → shop → service → slot picker → payment) collapses to 2 (home → payment). Information-first replaces sequential disclosure.

🎯

"Make requirements less dumb"

Q: "Why must customer click 3 levels deep to discover availability?" A: There is no reason. The data is in atizrail already. The card was the dumb requirement.

📊

"Question every requirement"

Old card requirements: "show photo + name". Q: who said? Booking.com/Airbnb/Uber all show 4-6 data points on the card. We were below industry baseline.

🔥

"Delete, delete, delete"

Deleted: shop browse flow (optional now) · 5 category tabs (replaced by 3 intent chips) · 800ms async-wait flash · alphabetical sort · "ทั้งหมด" default filter.

🚀

"Then accelerate"

Only after deletion. Precompute next-slot per shop on the backend (cache 60s). Sort/filter by precomputed field. No N+1 queries from the frontend.
