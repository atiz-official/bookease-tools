🎨 Stylistic decisions · cream chrome, white cards, warm but disciplined

Background does the work. Cards stay neutral white. No need for illustration noise.

Page background · gives stylistic warmth without competing with shop photos

The 7 design decisions in V5

Why each choice · how it serves the customer · how it differs from F
01

2 filter rows, not 3

Dropped the search bar — became a 🔍 icon in the top-right. Saves a row (~40px) for shop cards. Search is a low-frequency action; categories + time are high-frequency.

02

No "●" on selected chip

The black fill alone is unambiguous selection. The dot was redundant + reduced negative-space inside the chip. Apple/iOS pattern.

03

Warm cream chrome

#fbf5ec background + white cards. Familiar warmth (Airbnb · Booking.com · Squarespace) without being beige-grandma. Soft radial gradient tints at corners add depth.

04

2-col grid kept (current)

Familiar to your existing users. Doesn't force a learning step. Customer scans 6-8 shops per phone-height — same density as current Pinterest pattern.

05

5 info points per card (F upgrade)

Photo · availability pill · shop name · variant dot · category · ฿price · distance · heart. Current shows only photo + name + distance. 4× more decision-critical info, same space.

06

Distance moves to name row

"1.4km" rendered on the right of the shop name, tabular-num. Reads as "Rakkana Spa · 1.4km" in one glance. Category + price get their own line below, color-distinguished.

07

Stylistic eyebrow

Tiny italic "discover & book" above the wordmark · Cormorant Garamond serif · signals "this is curated, not a database dump." Pass 25 v37 already uses Cormorant on /payment trust strip · brand-coherent.
