✓ Pros · why it's good

  • Delight · personalization = emotional ownership = stickier customer
  • Differentiation · no competitor (Booking · Klook · Gowabi · Wongnai) does this
  • Low engineering cost · pure CSS variable swap · ~2-3 hr ship
  • Same UI pattern as merchant variant picker · brand-coherent · reuses muscle memory
  • Aligns with v3.drease.co precedent · we've done it before · know the gotchas

✗ Cons · why Elon would push back

  • Doesn't solve the customer's actual pain · finding shops · seeing availability · booking fast
  • Doesn't drive revenue · the green pill we just shipped DOES. This doesn't.
  • Conflicts with `feedback_illusion_of_customization` memory lock · "default to NO on customization"
  • Adds cognitive load · Pass 26 just stripped chrome to minimum. Adding choice = noise.
  • Photos are the visual anchor · v9 cream was picked precisely BECAUSE it lets photos pop. Bright themes (lemon · rose) fight the photos.

If you ship it anyway: 3 rules of taste — (1) ≤6 options, no color picker · (2) all neutral-leaning hues (no neon) · (3) hide behind a small icon, not in primary chrome. Default stays cream · 95% of customers never touch it · the 5% who do feel special.

🎯 If you ship it · 3 locked rules

These prevent the picker from becoming chaos noise.
  1. 6 themes max. No color picker, ever. Curated palettes only. "Customize your background" never means "pick any RGB."
  2. Cream stays default. All themes are warm-neutral (1 cool exception: Sky Blue). Never neon, never bright saturated. Photos always pop, chrome stays subtle.
  3. Variant B (icon + sheet) placement. NOT inline dots (steals prime real estate). NOT account-tab (too hidden). The 🎨 icon is the right friction level — visible enough to discover, hidden enough to not distract.
⚠️ Elon's true answer: ship the precise-time pill (Pass 26 v9.6 · just landed) and prove it lifts booking conversion FIRST. THEN add theme picker as a "delight cherry" in a quiet Pass 27. Don't dilute attention on the conversion lift with a personalization toy.
