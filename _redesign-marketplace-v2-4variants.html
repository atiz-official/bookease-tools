7 specific things Elon would kill in 60 seconds
FLAW 01

"📍 ใกล้คุณ 0 ร้าน" — while showing 12+ shops

Internal contradiction in the hero. Either lying about location or lying about results. Trust = 0.

FLAW 02

Cherry100 at 438.5 km shown to customer

Data quality leaks. Marketplace defense layer exists in shopQuality.ts but doesn't catch impossible distances.

FLAW 03

Cat-meme photo on "Kaiwat Shop"

Customer sees a confused cat in a Kamen Rider mask giving a thumbs up. Reads as: "BookEase is not curated."

FLAW 04

Hero takes ~40% of first viewport

"หาร้านสปา · จองได้ใน 60 วินาที" is marketing copy. Customer didn't open the app to read a slogan. Burns prime pixels.

FLAW 05

Zero availability signal anywhere

Photo + name + distance. Nothing about when you can actually book. The whole point of the product is missing from the card.

FLAW 06

Zero price signal

Is this ฿200 or ฿2000? Customer must click in to find out. Filters by budget impossible.

FLAW 07

Single category tab "ทั้งหมด"

One tab, underlined, no options. Why is it there? Either show filters or remove the tab. Indecision = noise.

🪞

My v1 was also wrong. You were right to reject it.

I shipped 7 elements per card (photo + avail pill + distance pill + name + category + price + 3 slot chips) plus 5 filter chips at top. That's not Elon — that's Salesforce. The instinct was correct (surface availability) but the execution added noise instead of removing it. The 3 variants below correct this — each shows 4 fields max, uses vertical density, and hides failure entirely.

The 6 Elon moves applied

First-principles applied to the marketplace · what changed between v1 and v2
🔥

Delete the hero

"หาร้านสปา · จองได้ใน 60 วินาที" is a slogan. Customer didn't open the app to read it. Reclaimed 40% of viewport for actual shops.

🚮

Hide failure

Cherry100 at 438km · cat-meme shops · shops with 0 availability — all filtered server-side. Never reach the customer. Failure is not their problem.

📊

Density over decoration

v1 had 7 elements per card. v2 has 4. Same info, fewer pixels. Vertical space rewards: 8 shops/screen vs 4 today.

⚡

1 tap = commit

The CTA on the row IS the booking action. Tap "19:05" → /payment with slot prefilled. Shop detail page becomes optional gallery, not gateway.

🎯

Default = signal

"ทั้งหมด" (noise default) → "⚡ ว่างตอนนี้" (signal default). Customer's most common intent answered by the default view.

🧭

Match the user's mental model

Customer thinks in time ("ตอนนี้" / "คืนนี้" / "พรุ่งนี้"), not alphabet. Variant C groups by time. Sort and section by intent, not by what's easy for the backend.
